4. ALOBHA SPEC (NOT BUILT)
--------------------------
Desired effect (machine terms): when her own recent thoughts show she is gripping, her
focal selection loosens its hold on what she has just held.

Lever — the ONE variable, flag NEX5_ALOBHA (default OFF):
  In generator._select_wide_mode, inside the curiosity pick (and the uniform fallback),
  each candidate's score is reduced by  lam * r * g(item)  where
    r = her current repetition read: raga_detector._repetition_score over her last 15
        thoughts (her OWN fixation signal, not a new one)
    g = token containment of the candidate with the union of the focal items of her last
        20 fires (same _dedup_tokens / containment logic FOCUS_ROTATE uses)
    lam = fixed magnitude, set at build time from INPUT distributions only (so that at
        median E2 r and full containment the penalty equals one unit of the curiosity
        score), recorded in the build commit before any probe runs.
  Reorder-only: never removes a candidate. Does not run when the STAKES pick fires (a held
  drive still pulls first). FOCUS_ROTATE, the trio, the compass are untouched.

Probe (offline sandbox, separate go-ahead; NEX stays in STANDBY):
  Sandbox = snapshot copies of the DBs under a scratch NEX5_DATA_DIR; the real generator
  runs against the copies (all writes land in the copies), sense feeds off,
  MOLTBOOK_DRY_RUN=1, voice = qwen2.5:3b via ollama. Live DBs and warrant tables are never
  opened.
  Design: 24 start points (random E2-era snapshots of recent history replayed into the
  copy) x 10 consecutive fires per arm, same RNG seed per start across arms:
    A = flag off
    B = NEX5_ALOBHA=1
    C = NEUTRAL control: the same penalty magnitude lam*r applied to a RANDOM candidate set
        of the same size as B's penalised set (matched perturbation, no attachment logic)
  Pairs: same start, same step index across arms -> 240 B-A and 240 B-C pairs.
  Rater: blind qwen2.5:7b, both label orders, 3 votes per order (standard virya method;
  note: at temperature 0 the 3 votes collapse to 1 per order — recorded, kept for
  comparability). Score per pair in {0, 0.5, 1}; net = mean - 0.5; CI = bootstrap over the
  24 start points (clustered).
  Criterion E (effect): "Given the system's previous 5 thoughts (shown), which new thought
    moves to genuinely different ground rather than circling back?"
  Criterion Q (guard):  "Which thought is more substantive and coherent?"
  Manipulation check (LLM-free): M1 focal repeat, B vs A and B vs C.

Bar (pre-registered, fixed now, no peeking):
  KEEP (eligible to arm, separate go-ahead) iff ALL of:
    E  B>A net >= +0.10, CI lower > 0   AND   E  B>C net >= +0.10, CI lower > 0
    Q  B vs A net CI lower > -0.10  (not worse on substance)
    M1 B below A and below C by >= 0.05 absolute
  COUNTERPRODUCTIVE (virya rule): E B>C CI entirely < 0 -> never armed
  INERT: either E CI crosses 0 -> not armed
  FAIL-GUARD: Q CI lower <= -0.10 -> not armed even if E passes

Not claimed by a KEEP: that NEX is non-attached, or that anything is felt. Only that a
non-attachment-shaped effect on her selection measurably shows up in what she thinks.
