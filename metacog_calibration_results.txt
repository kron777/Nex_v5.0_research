3. VERDICT
----------
FAIL. Pre-registered bar not met: primary dAUC -0.0009 (CI [-0.0021, +0.0001]) vs
the required >= +0.02 with CI > 0 and Brier skill > 0.

NEX's groove monitor carries no forward information about her next fire beyond her
own recent output record. Nothing she holds before a fire (groove alerts,
readiness, mood, surprise) predicts its outcome on its own (all AUCs 0.46-0.55).
hot_observer's "I notice this fire..." meta-beliefs are commentary on the fire,
not calibrated self-knowledge.

Decision per spec: do NOT build NEX5_METACOG_FORECAST. The honest null is the
result; it joins conviction / prasrabdhi / threadcarry as a measured non-effect.

Why the primary failed (descriptive, not a rescue): in Sep the alert stream is
saturated (97.9% of fires have an alert in the prior hour vs 63.9% in Jun-Aug),
so it can't discriminate. That is a property of GrooveSpotter's firing rate, and
it would need its own spec before anything is built on it.
