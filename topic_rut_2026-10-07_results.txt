THE FIX (built)
---------------
NEX5_PERSONA_FOCUS_ROTATE (default OFF), persona_responder.py only. After the SAME
interlocutor focus has been carried _PERSONA_FOCUS_STALE_TURNS (=4) consecutive
turns, the next would-be carry is forced off it: drift to the most frequent content
word in the reply that is NOT the stale focus, else clear the focus to "". A new
focus_streak column on interlocutor_state tracks the run; back-compat ALTER migrates
the existing live row. Token extraction was factored into _focus_tokens/_most_frequent
and reused by the forced drift (no new vocabulary rule invented). Nothing else
changed: the carry/drift logic below the bound, the mood walk, SOCIAL_N, the fountain
SOCIAL block, NEX's own fires. Fail-safe: any error returns the prior state.
