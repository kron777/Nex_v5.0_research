B. BINDING / INTEGRATION: does a synergizer synthesis depend jointly on BOTH parents?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data: beliefs.db synergizer_log, 6,984 of 7,494 syntheses with both parents surviving
(3,000 sampled); MiniLM embeddings on CPU.
Metric: movement toward each parent = sim(child, P) - sim(other parent, P), parents by
        logged ROLE (a/b), no selection on the child. Both-sided binding = both
        movements exceed placebo.
Baseline: placebo movement toward 10 random beliefs from the 7 days before each
        synthesis. Reference: a true 50/50 blend moves ~+0.44 toward each parent.
Result: toward B +0.1204 vs placebo +0.0595 -> +0.061 CI[+0.053, +0.069]
        toward A -0.0672 vs placebo +0.0058 -> -0.073 CI[-0.081, -0.065]
        both-sided 29.8% | one-sided 39.2% | near-copies (cos>0.95) 0.0%
        -> syntheses rewrite parent B and drift AWAY from parent A. One-sided.
(An earlier cut that labelled the "closer" parent by child similarity was discarded:
 it biases the other parent's similarity downward.)
Caveat for building: the synthesis text is written by the 3B. The metric is LLM-free,
but improving the property runs into the 3B ceiling.


A. SELF-TRANSITION INFORMATION: is there structure in her own state transitions, and
   does she hold any of it?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data: dynamic.db fountain_events hot_branch, 52,051 transitions (pairs <=30 min apart),
14 branch states, 145 days; leave-one-day-out.
Metric: held-out bits/transition saved by a first-order transition model.
Baselines: marginal; persistence; RECENT-MIX (last 20 fires) — plus a within-day
        shuffle null (destroys order, keeps the day's mix).
Result: marginal entropy 2.761 bits; stay rate 0.329.
        persistence over marginal  +0.034 CI[-0.011, +0.102]  (no fire-scale persistence)
        Markov over persistence    +0.459 BUT shuffle null +0.381 -> mostly day-mix
        Markov over RECENT-MIX     +0.048 CI[+0.026, +0.068]; shuffle null +0.027
        CI[+0.008, +0.045] -> genuine order structure ~0.02 bits (<1% of entropy)
Captured: her internal predictor's representative belief names the next fire's
        branch 24.6% (n=24,950) vs "same as last fire" 33.2% vs Markov upper bound
        44.3% -> she captures LESS than persistence.
Open: finer states (embedding clusters instead of 14 branches) are untested; choosing
