VERDICT UPFRONT
---------------
Pursue gap #1 (metacognitive calibration) first. It is the only one of the three
survey gaps that can be decided from data already on disk, with no LLM in the
loop (so no 3B ceiling), and it tests a capacity that NEX's own instruments
currently CLAIM without evidence (hot_observer's meta-beliefs; self_prediction's
"beats the coin").

Gaps #2 (broadcasting workspace) and #3 (homeostasis grounding valence) are
deferred because the two zero-build checks (section 1) show that the signals
they would build on are degenerate: internal "surprise" flags are 97.8% empty
windows, and valence sits near-constant at +0.73, making VALENCE_DRIVE a fixed
novelty bias rather than mood steering. Building broadcast or regulation on top of
those would amplify artifacts. They come back only after their inputs are fixed.

Honest framing: a PASS here means "an internal state she has before a fire carries
information about how that fire will go, beyond what her recent outputs already
show". That is functional self-monitoring. It is not evidence of awareness or
experience, and the write-up of any result must say so.

2. THE QUESTION (operational)
-----------------------------
Before fire t, does NEX's metacognitive state predict whether fire t will be a
template fire, better than (a) the base rate and (b) her own recent output record?

"Metacognitive state" = the input her Metacognition module (stage9) already reads:
GrooveSpotter's groove_alerts. This is the system's own representation of "I am in
a groove" — the closest thing she has to a confidence signal about her own
processing. (Readiness, the obvious alternative, is saturated and cannot carry
information; it is reported for the record only.)


3. DATA (all read-only, mode=ro; nothing new is logged for this step)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Unit:      one row of dynamic.db fountain_events with non-empty thought.
Windows:   PRIMARY      2026-09-01 .. 2026-10-03  (~11.7k fires, ~1.2k template)
           REPLICATION  2026-06-01 .. 2026-08-31  (held out; read only after the
                        primary result is written down)
Outcome:   Y_t = theory_x.stage_tom.hot_observer._is_template(thought_t),
           regex frozen at nex5 HEAD e40367e (record the regex sha256 in results).
           Base rate: 6.0% all-time, 10.3% last 30 days.
Primary predictor (ONE, pre-registered):
           G_t = severity-weighted count of beliefs.db groove_alerts with
                 detected_at in [ts_t - 3600, ts_t)   (strictly before the fire)
Persistence features (the baseline G must beat):
           P_t = { Y_{t-1};  template rate of the previous 10 fires;
                   template rate of all fires in [ts_t - 3600, ts_t) }
           (the last feature uses the SAME 60-min window as G, so G cannot win by
           being a lagged copy of the label — it must add beyond same-window output
           history)
Exploratory (reported, no pass/fail weight): readiness_t; valence/arousal (last
           affect_history row before t); last internal surprise score + empty-window
           flag; last drives_competing_log weights.


4. METRIC AND PASS BAR (pre-registered — fixed before the relationship is looked at)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Model A (baseline):   logistic(Y ~ P_t)
Model B (candidate):  logistic(Y ~ P_t + G_t)
Evaluation:  leave-one-day-out cross-validated probabilities (blocks by calendar
             day, so no fit sees its own day).
Metric:      dAUC = AUC_B - AUC_A   (type-2 discrimination added by the
             metacognitive signal); plus Brier skill of B over A.
CI:          day-block bootstrap, 2,000 resamples of days.

PASS:  primary window dAUC >= +0.02 AND 95% CI lower bound > 0 AND Brier skill > 0,
       AND replication window dAUC > 0 with CI lower bound > 0.
FAIL:  CI includes 0, or dAUC < +0.02, or replication flips sign.
       -> do NOT build. Record the honest null: her groove monitor carries no
       forward information beyond her recent outputs, so hot_observer's
       "I notice..." meta-beliefs are commentary, not calibrated self-knowledge.
Also reported (no weight): AUC of G alone, AUC of P alone, AUC of readiness alone
(expected ~0.5), calibration curve of B, and per-era dAUC across flag-arm dates.


5. THREATS AND HOW THE DESIGN HANDLES THEM
------------------------------------------
- Label circularity: GrooveSpotter's template_repetition alerts and the HOT regex
  look at overlapping phrasing. Handled by putting same-window output history in
  the baseline; G must add beyond it.
- Regime shifts (flags armed through Sep): day-blocked CV, per-era dAUC reported.
- Forking paths: one primary predictor, one window length (60 min), one label;
  everything else is exploratory and cannot rescue a FAIL.
- Anachronistic label: the regex grew from observed templates, so older data is
  scored by a newer regex. Acceptable for a retrospective test; it is the reason
  the decision rests on the primary (recent) window.
- Lookahead: only alerts with detected_at strictly < ts_t; P_t uses only prior
  fires. The prospective step below removes lookahead risk entirely.
- No LLM anywhere: label is a regex, predictor is substrate state. The 3B voice
  ceiling does not apply to this measurement.

  Add a dark, log-only forecast recorder (NEX5_METACOG_FORECAST, default OFF):
  at each fire it writes p(template) from Model B's frozen coefficients BEFORE the
  thought is generated, and resolves it after. Nothing reads the forecast; fire
  behaviour is unchanged. The single variable changed is "a forecast row exists",
  which turns the retrospective result into a PROSPECTIVE test (the prediction is
  committed before the outcome exists). Prospective pass bar = same dAUC rule vs
  same baselines over >= 14 days of live fires.
  Using the forecast for CONTROL (e.g. pre-emptive focus rotation) is a separate,
  later Phase-B decision and out of scope here.
